# Abstract

## E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Demand Prediction System

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, understanding sales patterns and predicting future demand is crucial for business success. This project presents an **E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Demand Prediction System** that leverages data science and machine learning techniques to analyze historical sales data and forecast future product demand.

The system processes 5,000+ e-commerce orders spanning 2023-2024, covering 5 product categories, 40 products, and 5 regions. Through comprehensive exploratory data analysis (EDA), we identify key trends including seasonal demand patterns, top-performing products, and regional sales variations.

Two machine learning models were implemented and compared:
1. **Linear Regression** - serving as the baseline model
2. **Random Forest Regressor** - providing improved prediction accuracy

The models predict future product demand based on features including price, category, region, and temporal attributes. Performance evaluation using MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R² metrics demonstrates the effectiveness of the Random Forest model in capturing non-linear demand patterns.

Key findings include:
- Winter season (Nov-Dec) shows 30-50% higher demand
- Electronics category generates highest revenue
- West region outperforms others in sales volume
- Price and product category are most influential features

The system generates actionable business insights and inventory recommendations, enabling data-driven decision-making for inventory management and profit optimization.

**Keywords:** E-Commerce Analytics, Demand Prediction, Machine Learning, Random Forest, Sales Forecasting, Data Science, Inventory Management
