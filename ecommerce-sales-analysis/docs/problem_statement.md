# Problem Statement

## E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Demand Prediction System

### The Problem

E-commerce businesses face significant challenges in managing inventory due to unpredictable customer demand. The key issues include:

1. **Overstocking:** Holding excess inventory ties up capital and increases storage costs
2. **Understocking:** Running out of popular products leads to lost sales and customer dissatisfaction
3. **Seasonal Variations:** Demand fluctuates significantly across seasons and holidays
4. **Regional Differences:** Sales patterns vary across different geographic regions
5. **Product Mix Optimization:** Identifying which products to stock and in what quantities

### Current Challenges

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Manual inventory management | Inefficient resource allocation |
| Lack of demand forecasting | Stockouts or overstock |
| No data-driven insights | Poor business decisions |
| Reactive approach | Missed opportunities |

### Proposed Solution

This project develops a data-driven system that:
1. Analyzes historical sales data to identify patterns
2. Uses machine learning to predict future demand
3. Provides actionable business insights
4. Enables proactive inventory management

### Scope

The system covers:
- Sales data analysis across multiple dimensions
- Demand prediction using ML algorithms
- Visualization of trends and patterns
- Business recommendations for inventory optimization
